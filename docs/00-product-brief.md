# DRIVE Product Brief

## Product

DRIVE is a gamified junior rowing erg training app for athletes aged 14 to 18.

The preferred first product is **DRIVE: Winter Quest**: a winter training experience where athletes complete coach-set erg quests, upload evidence, reflect on the session, and unlock rewards only after coach verification.

## Core Philosophy

DRIVE must reward execution quality, not just completion.

The app exists to make good rowing training habits visible, valued, and coachable. It should help athletes care about how they execute a session, not only whether they can post a fast split or collect metres.

## What DRIVE Rewards

- Pacing discipline
- Rate control
- Consistency
- Honest uploads
- Useful reflection
- Improvement over time
- Good training habits
- Coach verified effort

## What DRIVE Must Avoid

- Pure speed leaderboards
- Rewarding only fastest splits
- Rewarding only metres
- Meaningless badges
- Childish gimmicks
- Becoming a generic workout tracker
- OCR in the MVP
- Concept2 Logbook API in the MVP
- Live PM5 Bluetooth in the MVP
- Unity in the MVP

## MVP Loop

1. Coach creates weekly erg quests.
2. Athlete completes the erg session.
3. Athlete uploads a PM5 screen photo.
4. Athlete adds a short reflection.
5. Coach verifies or rejects the upload.
6. Rewards unlock after coach verification.
7. Squad mission progress updates.
8. River Map or Boathouse progress updates.

## Core Game Systems

- Today's Quest
- PM5 screen upload
- Athlete reflection
- Coach verification
- XP and badge rewards
- Athlete attributes: Engine, Discipline, Rhythm, and Grit
- Squad missions
- River Map progress
- Boathouse progress

## Product Standard

Every feature must answer this question:

> Does this help junior rowers build better training habits through honest, coach verified execution?

If the answer is no, the feature does not belong in DRIVE: Winter Quest.

## Warning For Future Agents

Do not reinterpret DRIVE as a generic fitness app, a leaderboard app, a rowing data platform, or a mini-game collection. The MVP is a focused coach verified training loop. Build around the brief, not around whatever technology seems interesting.
