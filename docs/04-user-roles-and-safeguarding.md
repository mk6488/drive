# User Roles And Safeguarding

## Audience

DRIVE is for junior rowing athletes aged 14 to 18 and the coaches who support them.

The product must be designed with safeguarding, privacy, and healthy training behaviour in mind.

## Roles

### Athlete

Athletes can eventually:

- View assigned quests
- Submit a PM5 screen photo
- Add a short reflection
- See verified rewards and progress
- Contribute to squad missions
- View River Map and Boathouse progress

Athletes should not be able to verify their own submissions.

### Coach

Coaches can eventually:

- Create weekly erg quests
- Review athlete uploads
- Verify or reject submissions
- Add clear rejection reasons or coaching notes
- Monitor squad mission progress

Coach actions affect rewards, so coach permissions must be treated carefully.

### Admin

Admin access may be needed later for managing clubs, squads, and coaches. Do not add admin features unless explicitly requested.

## Safeguarding Principles

DRIVE must avoid patterns that create pressure, humiliation, or unsafe training behaviour.

Design decisions should support:

- Coach oversight
- Private handling of junior athlete data
- Age-appropriate language
- Healthy training habits
- No public shaming
- No pressure to train through illness, injury, or exhaustion

## Public Visibility

The MVP should avoid public athlete profiles and public performance rankings.

Squad progress can be shared within the squad context, but it must not expose unnecessary personal data.

## Reflections

Reflections should be short and training-focused. They are not a mental health journal and should not ask athletes to disclose sensitive personal information.

Useful prompts can ask:

- What went well?
- What was hard to control?
- What would you try next time?

## Evidence Uploads

PM5 photos are training evidence. Treat them as private squad data.

Do not introduce social sharing, public galleries, or automatic analysis in the MVP.

## Coach Verification

Verification must be auditable and respectful. Rejection should include a practical reason where possible, such as:

- Wrong session uploaded
- PM5 screen unclear
- Missing reflection
- Session did not match the quest

Avoid blame-heavy wording.

## Agent Warning

Do not add public social features, direct messaging, public rankings, or extra data collection for junior athletes without explicit product approval and safeguarding review.
